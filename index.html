Riddle #1

Hear ye, hear ye

Each day the court jester poses one riddle. Reveal its answer letter by letter, then solve it before your lives run out.

  • You have 6 lives.
  • Consonants are free if they're in the answer. A wrong consonant costs 1 life.
  • Vowels (A E I O U) always cost 1 life, right or wrong. You buy them. You can't buy a vowel with your last life.
  • Press Solve at any time to guess the whole answer. A wrong solve costs 1 life.
  • Fill every tile or solve it to win. Your score is the number of lives you used, so lower is better.

Green = consonant found · Gold = vowel bought. Your keyboard works too: type letters, press Enter to solve.
