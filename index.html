The Gauntlet

Riddle after riddle until thy lives run out. Lives carry from one riddle to the next. Every solve heals 1 life, and a flawless solve heals 2. Streaks multiply thy points.

    Speak thy answer

    Hear ye, hear ye

    The court jester poses riddles. Reveal each answer letter by letter, then solve it before thy lives run out.

    • Guess any letter. If it's in the answer it's free and every copy flips over. A wrong letter costs 1 life.
    • Press Solve at any time and spell the whole answer on the scribe's keyboard. Every letter can be typed there. A wrong solve costs 2 lives, so guess with care.
    • Points: Squire 100, Knight 200, Sage 300. You get up to 3× that for solving while tiles are still hidden, plus 10 per life left, all multiplied by your streak.

    Two ways to play

    • Daily Riddle: one shared riddle a day with 6 lives. Squire on Mondays, Knight Tuesday to Thursday, Sage Friday to Sunday. Share thy result.
    • The Gauntlet: endless riddles with lives carried over. A solve heals 1 life and a flawless solve heals 2, up to 9. Yield skips a riddle for 1 life.

    Points become gold for the Armory: new skins and more. Your own keyboard works too: type letters to guess, press Enter to solve.
