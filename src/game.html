How to play

Read the riddle. Reveal the answer letter by letter — then solve it before you run out of lives. A new riddle every day.

  • You have 6 lives.
  • Consonants are free if they're in the answer. A wrong consonant costs 1 life.
  • Vowels (A E I O U) always cost 1 life — right or wrong. You buy them. You can't buy a vowel with your last life.
  • Tap Solve any time to guess the whole answer. A wrong solve costs 1 life.
  • Fill in every tile or solve it to win. Your score is the lives you used, so lower is better.

Green = consonant hit · Gold = bought vowel. Your physical keyboard works too: type letters, press Enter to solve.
